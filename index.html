01 / About

The short version.

Senior in Aerospace Engineering at Embry-Riddle, graduating December 2026. I like the part of a project where requirements turn into hardware: sizing the engine, drawing the parts, and proving the numbers with analysis and code.

Right now I split my time between Collins Aerospace, where I write Python test automation, author and verify test procedures for transponder products, and benchmark an internal AI test-case generation tool, and our senior capstone, where I lead a six-person team designing a crewed two-stage LOX/CH₄ lunar vehicle. On the capstone I own the engine operating point, nozzle design and OpenFOAM CFD, turbopumps, injectors and preburners, thrust vector control, mass properties, and all of the Onshape CAD and drawings.

Before that I built Python/PyQt5 mission-visualization tools at NASA Johnson Space Center used in controller training and simulated missions, including a 13× speedup of the Gateway tool, and spent a summer doing airframe CAD and an Unreal Engine 5 + PX4 flight simulator for a heavy-lift UAV.
