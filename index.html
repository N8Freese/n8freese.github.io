About me

A little more about what I do.

I'm currently finishing my final semester at Embry-Riddle while continuing to work part-time as a Systems Engineering Intern at Collins Aerospace. During the internship I've learned a lot about the systems engineering process, particularly requirements verification, test procedure development, and system-level testing. My work has included developing Python automation for transponder testing, evaluating an internal AI test-case generation tool, and supporting the sustainment of existing avionics systems.

Outside of the internship I'm leading a six-person senior design team developing a conceptual two-stage methalox launch vehicle for a crewed lunar mission. This project has been one of my favorite experiences in college because it has let me apply what I've learned in the classroom to a much larger engineering problem. My contributions have focused on propulsion analysis, engine and nozzle design, CFD, thrust vector control, mass properties, and CAD development.

I've also been involved in a heavy-lift UAV project, where I've worked on structural CAD models and on a flight simulation environment built with Unreal Engine 5 and PX4. Before that I interned at NASA Johnson Space Center through KBR and LZ Technology, where I developed Python-based mission visualization tools used to support flight controller training and simulated missions.

Through these experiences I've developed a strong interest in the overlap between aerospace design, systems engineering, and hands-on testing. I enjoy understanding how individual components work together as part of a larger system, and I'm always looking for ways to expand my technical knowledge.
